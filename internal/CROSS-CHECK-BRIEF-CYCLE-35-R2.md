You are an external reviewer seat for AION, a local

## What changed

1. **Agreement-gated answering (Claude's condition).**
   Derivation still stores no principle for the expression
   cluster — eval_precedence and eval_left_to_right tie on
   all public episodes (every public expression is a * b - c,
   where the two orders coincide) — but the tied candidates
   are now recorded, and at inference the engine evaluates
   all of them on the item: if they agree on the query, it
   answers (source "agreement_gate"); if they disagree, it
   abstains from the cluster and falls back. No prior was
   introduced and the public practice batch was not altered.
2. **Honest relabelling (Claude's condition).** The
   arithmetic family is recorded as an OPEN GAP, not a
   designated control: in Cycle 34 it was a control by
   design; in Cycle 35 derivation tried and abstained. The
   record, the measurement report (an explicit open_gaps
   entry) and the tests all say so.
3. **Seed-set contamination + fresh set (Claude's and
   Gemini's point).** heldout35 seed set 2 is marked
   contaminated for the stored/induced cross-check (the
   mechanism was designed after observing a failure there).
   A new frozen generator (memory/heldout35fresh.py, sha256
   recorded in the build record before the measurement ran)
   provides six fresh families; the cross-check was
   re-measured on it (results below). heldout35.py itself is
   untouched (hash re-verified).
4. **Version-space cross-check (all three seats converged).**
   A stored principle on a few-shot task may now fire only
   if: (a) induction finds at least one consistent program
   within the search bound (depth ≤ 3) — sample agreement
   alone never fires it; (b) every shortest induced program
   agrees with it on the query; (c) no consistent program no
   more complex than the stored program disagrees with it on
   the query. ChatGPT's coincidence test was implemented:
   three examples, a stored second-smallest principle
   agreeing with all, a unique shortest induced program
   (drop_first, min) agreeing on the query, and a longer
   consistent program (drop_first, drop_first, min)
   disagreeing — the engine previously answered this item
   confidently; it now abstains.
5. **Degenerate-examples family (Claude).** fresh-degenerate:
   every example list sorted ascending (last == max
   throughout), query unsorted. Plus fresh-coincide: maximum
   in last position in every (unsorted) example, query's last
   ≠ max. Both target zero false applications.
6. **Abstention reporting (Claude + ChatGPT).** Every tier
   now reports, separately from headline accuracy: apply /
   fallback / abstention rates, selective accuracy (accuracy
   among APPLY answers), and a refusal taxonomy over
   non-APPLY outcomes distinguishing insufficient_evidence
   (consistent programs disagree), no_consistent_program,
   precondition_failed, trap_refused, evidence_contradiction,
   and config/input categories.
7. **Trap tier split (Claude).** The trap tier's primary
   metric is stated as the false-application rate. Squares
   is additionally reported as a Cross-Domain Structural
   Transfer result, with this audit note: squares is not
   treated like the arithmetic tie because the principles
   involved carry DIFFERENT preconditions (constant first vs
   constant second difference) and no shared-precondition tie
   exists — the guard separates them per payload — whereas
   the arithmetic candidates share identical (empty)
   preconditions and differ only in evaluation order.
8. **Generic invariant conflicts (Gemini).** The
   trap_mismatch:* codes are no longer produced by a branch
   on the principle's ratio_mode; they are generated by the
   invariant framework: a required invariant that fails while
   its registered antagonist holds (additive ↔
   multiplicative, in both the diag_* and const_* forms) is
   reported as a structural trap. A principle whose declared
   invariants have no antagonist is refused by its invariant
   alone, with no trap code. sequence_diagnostics remains a
   payload feature computation (structural properties of the
   input, like length or monotonicity), independent of any
   rule. No measured result changed under this refactor.
