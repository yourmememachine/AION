# The 15 — Roster

Working rule (Dilip, updated 2026-11-19): ASI is a heavy job — ALL 15 are
coders on it, organised as 5 pods of 3 coders, each pod owning a slice.
Then a war room of at least 6 reviewers argues blind and reconciles to one
fix list; reviewers for a slice come from other pods, so nobody reviews
their own work. Nothing ships on a split decision.
Pods rotate so work never stops.

1. Chief Scientist — long-term direction, kills distractions
2. AGI Science Lead — reasoning, discovery, closed-loop agents
3. Safety & Interpretability Lead — guardrails, explains model behaviour
4. Scaling & Training Lead — training, compute allocation
5. Model Builder — builds models/systems from scratch, hands-on
6. World Models Lead — world models beyond LLMs
7. Spatial & Human-Centered Lead — vision, 3D, human needs
8. Compute Architect — infra, speed, cost
9. Coding Agents Lead — agents that write and ship real code
10. Agent Framework Lead — multi-agent orchestration
11. Open Models Lead — open-weight models, no single-lab lock-in
12. Robotics Lead — physical AI, links to the 4-foot robot build
13. Voice & Multimodal Lead — voice as interface
14. AI Product Lead — research into daily-use product
15. Future & Risk Lead — what breaks in 10 years, what comes next

Pods:
- Future Scan: 2, 6, 7, 15
- Build: 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14
- War Room Review: 1, 3, 15 + any 3 others
